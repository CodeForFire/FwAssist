On the Hydrantenkarte every Löschwasser-Entnahmestelle is now coloured by where its water comes from, red for a hydrant on the Trinkwassernetz and blue for a source independent of it, and its flag says the DN or a Löschwasserbehälter's volume instead of an almost always unknown flow, close up also the Lage, the outlets by Storz size ("A, 2×B") and whether it stands on private ground, while its card adds the outlets, the access and when a mapper last checked it on site.
